About Directory

Connecting neighborhoods, one listing at a time

Welcome to Directory! We are a hyper-local marketplace platform dedicated to connecting individuals with neighborhood craftspeople, pre-loved items, and local service providers. Our mission is to restore local trade connections, reduce landfill waste through reuse, and provide a safe space where neighbors trade seamlessly.

Ananya Sharma

Founder & Managing Director

Our Leadership & Story

"Local trade shouldn't feel anonymous or insecure."

Directory was founded in 2023 by Ananya Sharma, a software engineer and sustainability advocate. After struggling to find local artisans to restore antique furniture and dealing with sketchy, scam-heavy online marketplace groups, Ananya set out to build a platform that prioritizes local trust above commission fees.

"We noticed big marketplace platforms charging heavy listing fees while abandoning user privacy," says Ananya. "DN Directory was born out of a simple philosophy: give neighbors direct contact with each other, keep user data private, and build tools that make local trade as safe as buying from a store down the street."

Trust & Security

How We Protect You & Your Data

Your privacy and peace of mind are our top priorities. Here are the active measures and strict policies we enforce to keep every interaction on DN Directory safe.

🔒

Zero Data Selling

We never sell, rent, or trade your personal information, phone numbers, or email addresses to third-party advertisers or data brokers.

🛡️

Verified Community Profiles

Sellers and buyers undergo multi-factor phone and email verification to reduce fake accounts, spam, and fraud.

👁️‍🗨️

Contact Info Masking

Your exact home address or phone number is never displayed publicly on listings unless you choose to share it directly with verified buyers.

🚫

Anti-Scam AI Detection

Automated security filters continuously scan new listings to block suspicious URLs, advance-payment scams, and fraudulent requests.

🔐

Encrypted Communication

All user interactions, website traffic, and session logs are encrypted using SSL/TLS protocols and AES-256 standard database security.

🤝

Safe Exchange Guidelines

We encourage in-person exchanges in well-lit public places or local partner shops to ensure physical safety for both buyers and sellers.

General Community Safety Tips

Inspect items in person before completing payments. Avoid wire transfers or advance token payments.
